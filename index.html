MEET DJ DYME

DJ Dyme is a seasoned touring DJ, radio host, and former member of the Take Over DJs coalition. Former manager of the Piga Picha Crew and a staple on the airwaves, he currently rocks SPiNZ FM and hosts his decade-long syndicated franchise, Pull Up Fridays.
