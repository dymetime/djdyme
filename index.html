OFFICIAL PROFILE

MEET DJ DYME

DJ Dyme is a seasoned touring DJ, radio host, and former member of the Take Over DJs coalition. From dominating Atlanta's nightlife with five weekly residencies to rocking festival stages and national tours alongside headlining artists, Dyme brings undeniable energy to every stage he touches. Former manager of the Piga Picha Crew and a staple on the airwaves, he currently rocks SPiNZ FM and hosts his decade-long syndicated franchise, Pull Up Fridays. At his core, Dyme is a dedicated music curator driven by crowd connection and authentic sound.
